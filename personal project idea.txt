simplified

simulate in the abstract the relationship between workers and the player's mine which reflects the player's actions in an emergent way through interrelated systems.

workers working in a factory generate revenue, that revenue is given to the factory (the player) to be used as they see fit (reinvestment, wages, fixed costs) the player will be able to raise and lower wages to keep more or less of the surplus value to themselves, the amount of money available to workers in the form of wages effects their material conditions

the workers themselves will be individual nodes that act within constraints defined by their conditions in regards to the player's mine. 
There will have to be a clock to structure the logic, the game should begin with a given number of workers and a proportion of those workers will be 'born' (come of age to work in the mine) and will 'die' of natural causes (become too old to work in the mine) on a regular cycle. worker nodes all have a timer that decides when they 'die' of old age. workers could have a nutrition parameter that scales the rate their death timer drops at and decreases some specific revenue factor variable. 
workers could have an experience value that scales with time spent working & their productivity modifier.

sort of structured stream of consciousness:

something very systems heavy to tell the modern industrial story of kernow in a condensed and carefully arranged microcosm exploring the consequences of rapid industrialisation on kernowyon society & its landscape

Mine & village simulation loosely modelled on Geevor & St Just but presented as a sort of platonic ideal 'universal kernowyon coastal mining village' a la how the fictional village of Tassing is presented in the Obsidian title Pentiment

The mine is under control of the player while the village is autonomous and makes what decisions it can
the village will be a practical and diagetic form of feedback to the player, its state reflecting the choices and knock on effects of the player's actions over the course of the game

at this stage im thinking maybe a weekly cycle for the main gameplay loop, monday -> saturday will be one phase of continuous gameplay characterised by daily sub-loops perhaps with pauses for events and the game will pause on sunday for
a retrospective & upgrade/advanced action menu.
alternately for a longer form experience a monthly or even quarterly cycle could be options.

Or maybe a weekly cycle system to preserve that small scale operational detail with a time skip where the mine owner travels & returns for whatever reason. 
alternately have that be the premise for the epilogue presenting the long term knock on effects of the short-term game, might be more practical for a 1st attempt.

also seeing that the period of time that the player/tycoon was heading the mine and making decisions was so short yet had such an impact on the town could be thematically strong.
the intrusion of this system of alien capitalist logic had through its transformative & accelerationist nature had a disproportionate developmental influence over the social & economic order that existed before
and despite taking place during a very short period of the capitalist(players) life had complete domination over the lives of the people who worked to make them wealthy. 
it can present the danger of complete sublimation to logical control to the player
